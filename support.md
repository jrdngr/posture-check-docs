# Posture Check Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: hotsoupmouth@gmail.com

## Common questions

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Check that notifications are on for Posture Check in the iPhone Settings app, that Reminders is on in the app's Settings, and that the time is within your active hours. A pause or a Focus that uses the Posture Check filter also holds reminders.

**How do I get the Apple Watch app?**
Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find Posture Check under Available Apps, and tap Install.

**What is hard mode?**
A short burst of frequent reminders. Start it with the flame button, from the watch, or from Control Center. It can also start after a Bad rating (Settings → Hard mode). Hard mode answers are left out of your stats.

**How do I back up or move my data?**
With iCloud backup on, a copy is saved to iCloud Drive each day. On a new iPhone, go to Settings → Restore iCloud backup and pick one. You can also export a CSV from History and bring it back with Settings → Import CSV. Restoring only adds ratings that aren't already in the app.
