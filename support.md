# Shrimply Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: hotsoupmouth@gmail.com

## Common questions

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Check that notifications are on for Shrimply in the iPhone Settings app, that Reminders is on in the app's Settings, and that the time is within your active hours. A pause or a Focus that uses the Shrimply filter also holds reminders.

**How do I get the Apple Watch app?**
Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find Shrimply under Available Apps, and tap Install.

**What is hard mode?**
A short burst of frequent reminders. Start it with the flame button, from the watch, or from Control Center. It can also start after a Bad rating (Settings → Reactive settings → Hard mode). Hard mode answers are left out of your stats.

**How do I back up or move my data?**
With iCloud backup on, a copy is saved to iCloud Drive each day. On a new iPhone, go to Settings → Manage iCloud backup and pick one. You can also export a CSV from History and bring it back with Settings → Import CSV. Restoring only adds ratings that aren't already in the app.

**How do I add a friend?**
On the Friends tab, tap + and send the invite link. Your friend needs Shrimply and an iCloud account. Once they accept, you each see the other's day.

**How do groups work?**
Make one in Settings → Friends → New Group, then invite people with your friends list, the group's link, or its QR code. Once a day, everyone in the group gets the same check at the same moment, and answers stay hidden until you answer.

**How do I remove a friend or leave a group?**
In Settings → Friends, swipe a friend to remove them, or open a group to leave it. Sharing stops right away.

**Friends isn't working.**
Friends uses iCloud, so check that you're signed in to iCloud in the iPhone Settings app. Everything else in Shrimply works without it.
