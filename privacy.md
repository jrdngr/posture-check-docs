# Posture Check Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Posture Check doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or third-party services, and the app doesn't talk to any server run by the developer.

## Your data

- **Ratings** you log, and your settings, are stored on your iPhone.
- **Apple Watch:** the watch app sends your ratings and settings straight to your iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity.
- **iCloud backup:** when it's on (Settings → Backup), a copy of your ratings is saved once a day to your own iCloud Drive, in the Posture Check folder. The developer can't see it. You can turn it off at any time.
- **Notifications** are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server.

## Sharing

Your data leaves the app only when you choose to share it, for example by exporting a CSV from History.

## Deleting your data

- History → Delete All removes every rating.
- Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
- To remove backups, delete the Posture Check folder in the Files app under iCloud Drive.

## Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
