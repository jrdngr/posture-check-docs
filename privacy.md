# Shrimply Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Shrimply doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or third-party services, and the app doesn't talk to any server run by the developer.

## Your data

- **Ratings** you log, and your settings, are stored on your iPhone.
- **Apple Watch:** the watch app sends your ratings and settings straight to your iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity.
- **iCloud backup:** when it's on (Settings → Backup), a copy of your ratings is saved once a day to your own iCloud Drive, in the Shrimply folder. The developer can't see it. You can turn it off at any time.
- **Notifications** are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server.
- **Widgets and the Live Activity** show today's % Good and ratings on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch. The Friends Today widget also shows the names of your friends and group members, and your friends' days. They read from the app on your iPhone.

## Friends

Friends is optional. It uses Apple's iCloud sharing (CloudKit), so it only works with people you invite, and the developer can't see any of it.

When you add a friend, they can see:

- the name you enter in Settings → Friends
- today's ratings and their times
- your % Good for each of the last 7 days, and your weekly league points
- your time zone
- your graduation stage, and whether your reminders are on or paused
- the groups you're both in, and your answers to this week's group checks
- your milestones (stage-ups and bursts ended early), only if you turn on See my milestones

Friends can send you a check (one reminder), a reaction, or a spot (a Bad logged for you). Turn off Log spots to stop spots, and you can dispute any spot. Each kind of notification from friends can be turned off in Settings → Notifications.

Everything you share is kept in your own iCloud account and is shared only with the friends you add. Remove a friend in Settings → Friends to stop sharing with them.

## Groups

A group's members can include people who aren't your friends. Members who aren't your friends see only the name you enter in Settings → Friends (or, as with any iCloud share, the name on your Apple Account if you haven't entered one) and your answers to that group's checks this week.

Whoever makes a group can make other members its admins. The maker and admins can:

- **Turn on the group's link**, which anyone can open to ask to join. Asking goes through Apple's iCloud sharing. The maker and admins see your iCloud name, and a short emoji code worked out from your iCloud account and the link. Apple's sharing also sends their phones the email address or phone number of your Apple Account, but the app never shows it. They let you in, or approve each request, and can remove anyone, who then can't join by the link again.
- **Show a QR code**, a one-time invite to the group. It's made fresh each time, and taken back if it's closed before anyone uses it.
- **Invite their friends.** You see the group's name and who invited you, and join only if you accept. Until then the maker and admins see your invite as open; declining takes it back.

You can leave any group in Settings → Friends.

Anyone in a group can ask you to be friends with Add Friend. Nothing more is shared until you accept; if you decline, the request is deleted.

## Sharing

Apart from Friends, your data leaves the app only when you choose to share it, for example by exporting a CSV from History.

## Deleting your data

- History → Delete All removes every rating.
- Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
- To remove backups, delete the Shrimply folder in the Files app under iCloud Drive.
- To stop sharing with friends, remove them in Settings → Friends.

## Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
